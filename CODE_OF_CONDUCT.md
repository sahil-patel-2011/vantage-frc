# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Vantage a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

This includes FRC students, mentors, parents, and anyone cloning the repo.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the project and for teams using it
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (addresses, emails, secrets, student
  data) without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  school or FIRST Robotics setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents to **sahiljpatel2011@gmail.com**. Do not file a public GitHub
issue for harassment, student data, or leaked credentials.

Project maintainers will review and respond as they are able. Maintainers may
remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct,
and may ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests,
discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
