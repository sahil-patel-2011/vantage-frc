## Verdict

Vantage has more FRC surface area than every competitor combined and less working depth than several single-purpose free tools. The numbers: 216 page directories, 327 API routes, 289 migration files, 184 compute modules — and of the ~87 features audited in depth, roughly 10 rate SOLID (about 11%), 14 are outright BROKEN, 13 are FRAGMENTS, and the remaining ~50 are THIN. The dominant failure mode is not bad code; it is islands. Feature after feature owns a private table that no other compute file reads: eight separate pick-list models, five money ledgers, three quantity stores, three pre-match briefings, three weight systems, four safety/training systems, two task boards shipped as sibling nav entries. Data goes in and nothing downstream consumes it.

Against the owner's goal specifically: custom scouting forms exist but with eight field types and no counter, stopwatch, or rating — the three things an FRC scout actually does — and renaming a question silently orphans all prior data. Pit photos capture and store correctly and then fail at every last mile (no thumbnail, no EXIF rotation, entry_id never written, no gallery anywhere). Local caching is the sharpest gap: the text outbox is genuinely good, but images are never cached at all, and two independent bugs permanently wedge the outbox past 100 entries with the error swallowed by an empty catch — a team could lose a full regional's data. CAD document upload does not exist in any form (no file input, no multipart handler, no bytes). 3D printing management does not exist. Spare parts exists five times over and the failure→consumption→reorder→receipt loop is open at both ends; the forecast is arithmetically inert five months a year and reports "no risk" today regardless of stock. "Everything AI led" is the largest honesty gap — 37 features wear an AI badge over deterministic templates with costUsd 0, chat never sends conversation history to the model, and every real AI route can be killed mid-transaction because the function duration is left at the platform default. AI CAD has exactly two mutating primitives while eight more are declared and unimplemented, and the whole brief→plan→execute pipeline is reachable from no page. Bugbot reviews 8 files of a 40-100 file repo and, after a repo scan, the paid $2 fix runs against the wrong source. Chat has one hardcoded channel and loads the oldest 100 messages first — no team leaves Discord for that.

What is genuinely strong, and it is the strongest thing here: tenancy. Org-scoped RLS, the vantage_app/vantage_worker split with lint enforcement, admin audit, closed provisioned membership, envelope-encrypted BYO keys, and metered AI with a real ledger. Lookout for FRC attempted this same all-in-one thesis, could not answer the youth-PII question, and has been on a waitlist since January 2024. Vantage can answer it. The honest read is that the all-in-one bet is still live and still unclaimed, but the product is currently about 30% of the way to being usable by a real team for a full season — and the gap is closed by finishing and connecting what exists, not by adding surfaces. One more thing worth saying plainly: this market prices at $0. Every durable tool in it is free. The monetizable ground is metered AI, CAD/manufacturing workflow, and the compliance posture that makes a district say yes — nothing on the scouting side will ever be paid for.

## Broken (fix first)

### Offline outbox and sync (lib/scout-offline.ts)

It does NOT survive a full day with no wifi. Two independent bugs wedge the outbox permanently, and there is no UI to recover from either. (1) HARD 100-ENTRY CEILING WITH NO CHUNKING. `syncOutboxOnce` posts every pending entry in one request (scout-offline.ts:271-280) and the route rejects `entries.length > 100` with a 400 (api/scouting/sync/route.ts:15-17). Six scouts x 60+ qual matches is routinely 300+ rows. `withSyncBackoff` does not distinguish a permanent 4xx from a transient failure (sync-backoff.ts:53-75), so it retries five times, throws, and the outbox never drains — every subsequent sync attempt fails identically. There is zero slicing anywhere in the codebase (verified by grep). (2) NO PER-ENTRY ERROR ISOLATION. The route loops `results.push(await repository.syncEntry(...))` with no try/catch (sync/route.ts:19-25), and `syncEntry` throws on any validatePayload failure (repository.ts:266-268). One poisoned row — a required field left blank, which the client never checks — aborts the entire batch and nothing gets acknowledged. Combined: a single skipped required field can cost a team an entire event's scouting data. There is also no outbox inspector: nothing in /scouting or /offline lists queued entries, lets a scout edit or delete a bad row, or shows which entry is blocking the batch. The only recovery is clearing browser storage, which destroys everything.

### Coverage and shift assignment

/scouting/lineup is completely non-functional. It polls `/api/scouting/coverage` on an interval (apps/web/app/scouting/lineup/lineup-client.tsx:253) and THAT ROUTE DOES NOT EXIST — `apps/web/app/api/scouting/` contains only bootstrap, disagreements, formulas, handoff, media, schemas, sync, trust (confirmed against `git ls-files`). Every poll hits the Next 404 HTML page, `response.json()` throws, and the page shows 'Network error — coverage will retry.' forever. This is in committed HEAD, not a working-tree regression. The page is also not merely orphaned: it is linked from the Form builder's related strip as 'Coverage' (`FORM_BUILDER_RELATED_LINKS`, form-builder.ts:335) and from the post-save receipt as 'Lineup & coverage' (`scoutingPostSaveNextSteps`, form-builder.ts:322-326) — two of the highest-traffic exits in the whole scouting flow.

### Scouting -> strategy and pick list consumption

The payload CONTENT does not arrive, because the bridge and the schemas do not share a key convention. `scout-ops.ts` resolves capabilities by exact camelCase key lookup with no normalization: AUTO_KEYS = auto|autoPoints|autoScore|autoPieces|autoCoral|autoNotes|mobility|autoMobility (:101-110), TELEOP_KEYS (:111-119), ENDGAME_KEYS (:120), SCORE_KEYS (:99), FOUL_KEYS (:100). But the SHIPPED DEFAULT match schema emits snake_case: `auto_score`, `teleop_score`, `endgame`, `disabled`, `notes` (packages/scouting/src/index.ts:110-118, mirrored in packages/game-year/src/index.ts:35-36). I ran the actual key-matching logic against the actual default payload: auto -> null, teleop -> null, endgame -> null. `endgame` IS in ENDGAME_KEYS but the default schema stores it as the string 'full'/'partial'/'none', which firstFinite rejects. So for a team using the out-of-the-box form, `deriveScoutCapabilities` returns all-null, `blendPrivateEpa` short-circuits with 'Scout entries have no auto/teleop/endgame fields to blend.' (private-edge.ts:135-141), and pEPA is permanently null. reliability and foulRate are null for the same reason. The ONLY things that survive the crossing are `notes` (NOTE_KEYS matches) and a `/defen[cs]e/` regex over the stringified payload. Custom forms are worse: slugifyKey always emits snake_case, so a builder question can only ever reach strategy if its label slugs to exactly one of `auto`, `cycles`, `climb`, `endgame`, `park`, `trap`, `mobility`, or `notes` — i.e. single-word labels only. 'Auto Points' -> `auto_points` -> invisible. A team can scout diligently all weekend and the pick desk will tell them their scouting has no blendable fields.

### Pick-list data model (cross-cutting)

There is no single pick list. picklist_collab_lists/_entries/_votes (0279_picklist_collab.sql:5,18,34) and alliance_selection_desk_sessions/_slots (0418_alliance_selection_desk.sql:6,22) are touched by nothing except their own compute file — verified by grep across apps/web and packages. A team that builds a weighted-consensus list in /picklist-collab gets zero effect on /pick-clock, /strategy/draft, or /picklist-justifier, all of which read pick_lists. Worse, the schemas cannot be joined: pick_list_entries.team_key is text FK to teams_ref (0004:57) while picklist_collab_entries.team_number is an integer (0279:22) and alliance_sim_robots.team_number likewise (0202_alliance_sim.sql:32). compute-picklist-collab.ts:203 papers over this at read time with a `/^frc(\d{1,5})$/i` regex to re-derive a team number from team_key. There are also two competing alliance-board implementations (alliance_boards jsonb state vs alliance_selection_desk_slots rows) with no migration or bridge between them.

### Pre-match briefing (three competing surfaces)

Three overlapping briefings, none a superset, overlapping only on matches_ref and match_strategies. /briefing uniquely holds whiteboard_plays, driver_sessions/driver_cycles practice readiness, video_reviews/video_notes and scout_assignments (api/briefing/route.ts:85,97,267,277,299). /match-copilot uniquely holds fmea_failures and battery health. /command uniquely holds pit flags, scout coverage and logistics. To walk to the field fully briefed a drive team must open at least three pages — and none of them shows the match strategy card, counter-book, defense plan, or watchlist notes the team actually wrote. hubs.ts:78-105 nests 26 tabs under the Strategy workbench alone, against docs/FEATURE_MAP.md:13's claim of 'not ten extra tools'.

### CAD document / STL import

There is no import at all. No file input, no multipart handler, no signed-upload flow anywhere under apps/web/app/cad, apps/web/lib/cad or apps/web/app/api/cad. A team with a robot already modelled in SolidWorks, Inventor, or an STL from a vendor has no way to get it into Vantage — Onshape OAuth (or Fusion on the user's own PC) is the only door. Even the export side stores no bytes: STEP/GLTF keep only a translationId and note that the user must download from Onshape (onshape.ts:411-425), so the 'artifact' a team gets is a pointer, not a file.

### Bugbot Ultra flat-fee billing (scan $1 / fix $2 / recheck $1)

After a repo scan, the paid $2 fix runs against the wrong source. apps/web/app/code/code-client.tsx:934 calls runBugbot({phase:"fix", scanRepo:false}), so the request body sends `content` = the textarea — which still holds CODE_COACH_SAMPLE (code-client.tsx:118) unless the user separately pasted code — together with `findings` from the repo scan whose evidence strings come from files that are not in that content. groundBugbotFix then drops the diff (its '-' lines cannot be found), the user is told "No grounded diff", and the $2 is charged anyway because chargeUsd is applied inside meteredAI's invoke regardless of outcome (api/code/route.ts:330). The recheck button (code-client.tsx:942) has the identical defect for $1.

### Team chat correctness / scale risks

Three concrete defects. (1) WRONG END OF HISTORY: the initial thread load passes `since = null` and the query is `ORDER BY m.created_at ASC LIMIT 100` (route.ts:603, :628). Opening a channel with 500 messages renders the OLDEST 100 — the season's first week — and the user must wait ~5 long-poll cycles (7.5s each) for the client to walk forward to today. There is no reverse pagination and no "load older" control. (2) NOTIFICATION FAN-OUT PER MESSAGE: route.ts:892 loops every org member and calls `emitPreferredNotification`, which itself runs a `SELECT notification_prefs FROM profiles` then an INSERT (packages/core/src/in-app-notifications.ts:99-107). A 40-member team pays ~80 sequential queries inside the POST transaction for every single chat message and accrues 40 inbox rows per message — a chatty build night writes tens of thousands of notification rows. (3) LONG-POLL HOLDS A DB CONNECTION: the wait loop sleeps and re-queries inside `withRls` (route.ts:1004-1012), so every viewer pins one Postgres connection for up to 7.5s and polls `hasThreadUpdates` every 1.2s. On serverless Postgres this collapses well before a single team is fully online. Separately, `enrichObjectLink` for `objectType === "knowledge"` ignores the supplied objectId entirely — `SELECT ... FROM team_knowledge WHERE org_id = $1 LIMIT 1` (route.ts:180) — so any knowledge link resolves to an arbitrary row.

### Money tracking (cross-cutting verdict)

Money is tracked in FIVE disconnected ledgers, not once: `finance_transactions` (orders/sponsors/fundraisers), `finance_purchase_log` (Season Finance Desk, 0434), `season_costs` (/costs, 0070), `purchase_requests` (/orders, 0035), `bom_cost_rollup_line_items` (0264). And FOUR budget records: `season_budgets.total_budget_usd`, `finance_budget_plans` (per category), `finance_season_settings.operating_budget_usd`, `bom_cost_rollup_budgets`. The canonical ledger `finance_transactions` is not read by the newest and most complete surface (lib/business/compute-season-finance.ts re-derives everything from source tables instead), so the two budget screens inside the SAME hub report different totals — app/business/business-client.tsx:534-535 literally renders 'Open season finance' and 'Open budget' as adjacent buttons to two incompatible views of the same season. There is no single answer to 'what have we spent'.

### Sponsor wall (/sponsor-wall)

It is described in its own UI as 'Build a public thank-you wall for your sponsors' (app/sponsor-wall/sponsor-wall-client.tsx:292) but there is NO public surface. `/sponsor-wall` is absent from PUBLIC_PAGES and `/api/sponsor-wall` is absent from PUBLIC_PREFIXES (apps/web/proxy.ts:7-60), so only logged-in team members can ever see it. There is no share link, no embed snippet, no token route — compare /showcase which does have /api/showcase/public and a get_showcase_snapshot function. `sponsor_wall_settings.published` and `sponsor_wall_entries.published` are toggles to nowhere. The entire purpose of the feature — sponsors and the community seeing their logo — is unreachable.

### Grant report (/grant-report)

The core number is wrong. A post-grant report tells a funder how THEIR money was spent, but lib/grant-report/compute-grant-report.ts:219-224 selects every `finance_transactions` expense for the whole org and season with no filter tying it to the grant, then presents that as the grant's spend-by-category. A team with a $2,000 grant and a $40,000 season will send the funder a report claiming $40,000 of grant spend. There is no schema support for doing it correctly (no grant_id on expenses, no restricted-fund model). Separately the same query misses `finance_purchase_log` and `season_costs` entirely, so the total is simultaneously inflated and incomplete.

### Showcase (/showcase, /showcase/present)

The 'Start reasoning Q&A practice' button is broken. app/api/showcase/route.ts:12 returns `{id, questions}` where `id` is a judge_practice_sessions id, and app/showcase/showcase-client.tsx:6 then calls `load(d.id ?? selected)` — which refetches `/api/showcase?deckId=<practiceSessionId>`, a nonexistent deck. The questions are never rendered, and the user's deck sections vanish from the editor. `judge_practice_sessions` is written and never read by any code in the repo. Separately: no loading state (a bare `message` string), no per-section save without approving, and both client files are single-line minified-density JSX that is effectively unmaintainable.

### AI route operational readiness (function timeouts)

The adapter waits 25s for an upstream that the serverless function will kill first - the default function duration is well under 25s unless declared. Every AI route can therefore be terminated mid-meteredAI transaction, and the 8-step autonomous loop cannot complete on defaults at all. The one place a long duration is declared is the cron path, which suggests the AI routes were simply never given one.

## Differentiators (lean into these)

- Real multi-tenant security as a shipped product feature, not a schema detail: org-scoped RLS on every table, separated vantage_app / vantage_worker DB roles (lint-blocked so request code cannot import dbAdmin), admin audit, closed provisioned membership, envelope-encrypted secrets. Lookout for FRC died on exactly this question with the answer 'our school's firewall'; Skedula was banned district-wide after a breach. This is the only card in the deck that unlocks school/district procurement, and it belongs on the marketing page.
- Structurally YPP-compliant chat: adult-student DMs that cannot exist unsupervised because the data model forbids them (auto-added second adult), plus tombstoned edits/deletes and a one-click exportable transcript formatted for a FIRST report. 61% of US public-school teams are blocked from something they want, and this is the named blocker. Nothing on the market ships it. Discord structurally cannot.
- Multi-season institutional memory. Slack free deletes at 90 days, Notion at 7, students graduate annually and take everything. A tenant that carries scouting history, decisions, part designs, vendor relationships and playbooks across four years of turnover is something no FRC tool offers and every mentor is quietly bleeding from.
- Metered AI with BYO-key and per-org cost control. PitPilot gates AI behind usage limits and charges $5.99/team to raise them — a hard ceiling on any free AI product. meteredAI + the usage ledger + BYOK envelope encryption lets a funded team spend its own money and a poor team stay free. That is a business-model differentiator the free ecosystem structurally cannot copy.
- Pit photos as a first-class, offline-captured, auto-tagged, cross-season object that appears next to a team everywhere else in the app. Named the most-used pit data by four independent mentors and broken in every tool including the best ones; a Feb 2026 Chief Delphi thread exists whose entire subject is 'nothing does this'.
- CAD-linked manufacturing and print status: Onshape/vault document -> BOM -> part status -> who's making it -> is the stock in the bin -> has it been ordered. WPI's robotics director is hand-building this in Baserow off the Onshape API and estimates a couple of years; CheesyParts proved it in 2013 Ruby and nobody modernized it. When a mentor posted a screenshot of an Onshape-embedded kanban, the next five replies were all 'please release this'.
- Cross-feature joins no other tool can make, because no other tool holds the data in one tenant: 'this subsystem is behind schedule, the part is stuck at QC, the replacement bearing was never ordered, and the two students who know the assembly haven't attended in three weeks.'

## Do not build (integrate instead)

- Event-day queuing, pit maps, live match schedules and volunteer workflows — Nexus for FRC is free, in use at the 2026 FIRST Championship, a Gold Supplier, and has webhooks. Consume its webhooks the way Pulse does; competing with it is competing with FIRST. Its pit-assignment API is also how Maneuver auto-routes pit scouts — use it for the same.
- A rating model. Statbotics' EPA is MIT-licensed, has a REST API and a Python package, and was independently the best FRC rating model in 15 of the last 20 years. Rolling your own is cost with negative credibility. Add the robot-action layer EPA structurally cannot see — EPA is derived from match results, lags real improvement by an event, and cannot say 'good from the left, weak in auto, plays defense'.
- An event/match/team database. TBA APIv3 plus the official FRC Events API are free, authoritative, webhook-capable and already the backbone of packages/reference. Keep the ETag/backoff ingest worker; raise event-day refresh above the single daily 14:00 UTC cron, which is not 'live' for match copilot or pick clock.
- Pit display screens. PitFUSION and Pulse are both free, both single-page, both read the same Nexus/TBA/Statbotics upstreams, and Nexus has its own built in. Ship a URL, not a product.
- 3D printer telemetry, slicer integration and G-code handling. OctoPrint, Bambu Handy, Prusa Connect and SimplyPrint own this, FRC teams run mixed fleets, and the vendor APIs churn constantly. Build the human layer nobody has — queue, ownership, filament accounting, part linkage — and at most link a print job to a part record.
- Replacing Discord outright. The shadow student Discord survives every attempt to kill it ('Nothing I've done or tried has gotten my students to stop using Discord. Nothing.'). Win the record-of-authority tier on compliance grounds and bridge announcements outbound into their Discord rather than demanding a migration.
- Youth registration, consent forms and YPP clearance — STIMS is FIRST-owned and mandatory. The most you should do is reconcile the roster against it and surface who is missing clearance.
- Docs, spreadsheets and general forms — Google Workspace for Education is free, IT-approved and already deployed. Be additive: import from Sheets, export to CSV, and never require a team to abandon it first.
- Authoring a rival scouting data interchange standard. The Purple Standard already tried with the largest collaborative dataset behind it and stalled at 6 stars. Adopt TPS for interop credibility and, per the community's own advice, build the converter rather than the standard.
- A generic kanban or Gantt. Trello's 10-collaborator cap created a real opening, but the opening is for robotics-shaped work — a task attached to a part attached to a CAD document attached to an order — not a better Trello.
- Physical bin/location tracking as a primary UX. 'The number of folks who will look in a spreadsheet regularly to nail down the location of something rounds to zero.' Labels and clear bins win; track quantity, cost and reorder point, which labels cannot do.
- A strategy whiteboard / field-drawing tool. The community verdict, from the thread where two developers were actively building one: print one field layout, laminate it, use whiteboard markers, and spend the season on something else. Both devs agreed.
- Yet another scouting form builder as the acquisition wedge. 585 forks of ScoutingPASS, 160 of QRScout, 363 teams on Scoutradioz, a dozen new apps every January. Scouting is table stakes you must have — it is not a reason anyone switches.

## The ordered build list

### 1. Make offline survive a competition weekend (outbox chunking, quarantine, media size guard, real cache measurement)  [L]

**Why:** A scout tablet at a 2-day regional routinely queues >100 entries. `apps/web/app/api/scouting/sync/route.ts:15-17` hard-rejects any batch over 100, and `syncOutboxOnce` (apps/web/lib/scout-offline.ts:271-278) posts the whole outbox in one request — so past entry 101 the outbox NEVER drains and retries the same 400 forever. Worse, the loop at sync/route.ts:21-24 runs inside one `withRls` transaction, so one bad entry rolls back every good entry behind it. Oversize phone photos (>6MB cap at media/[clientId]/route.ts:6, itself above Vercel's ~4.5MB body limit) queue permanently and burn the arena's only bandwidth. `syncMediaOutbox`'s `catch {}` (:365) hides all of it — the user sees a stuck pending count and no way to recover. Today teams route around this with paper and QRScout; a single lost regional's data ends the trial. E301 makes offline a rule, not a preference.

**Plan:** Migration `0443_offline_precache.sql`: `scout_outbox_quarantine` (org_id FK CASCADE, client_id, entry_type CHECK match|pit|media, payload jsonb, error_message, attempts, device_label, first/last_failed_at, resolved_at/by, reported_by, UNIQUE(org_id, client_id)); `offline_device_cache_state` (org_id, device_id, device_label, routes_cached text[], media_cached/expected int, bytes_used bigint, quota_bytes, storage_persisted bool, pending_entries/media, network_status CHECK online|offline|degraded, last_verified_at, reported_by, UNIQUE(org_id, device_id)); `offline_precache_manifests` (org_id, event_key, payload jsonb, media_count, route_count, total_bytes, generated_by, generated_at, expires_at).
